# Phase 6: Beta Launch and GA Readiness

## Objective

Run a controlled beta, close key gaps, and reach production launch readiness.

## In Scope

- Beta onboarding and support process.
- Funnel instrumentation from upload to insight consumption.
- Feedback intake and prioritized fix loop.
- Reliability/performance tuning based on real usage.
- GA readiness checklist and release criteria.

## Out of Scope

- Large architectural rewrites unless required for launch stability.

## Exit Criteria

- Activation and reliability targets are met.
- Billing, auth, and core analysis flows are stable.
- Team signs off on GA checklist.
